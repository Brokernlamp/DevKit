# DevKit Founder Recording Checklist

Use this before and during recording. Clip scripts are in [founder-recording-script.md](./founder-recording-script.md). Placement is in [editing-timeline.md](./editing-timeline.md).

## Setup

- [ ] Record in **portrait (vertical)** orientation, with the phone held upright. Do not record sideways.
- [ ] Use **2160x3840 (4K UHD)** at **30 fps** if your phone supports it. The minimum is **1080x1920 at 30 fps**. The composition is 1080x1920 at 30 fps, so 30 fps keeps the cuts smooth.
- [ ] Turn off HDR and slow motion.
- [ ] Lock focus and exposure on your face so the picture does not shift mid-clip.
- [ ] Use the main (rear) camera for better sharpness. Because you cannot see yourself through it, frame the shot with a helper or show the live preview on a second device. Use a tripod or a stable stand at eye level.
- [ ] Put the phone at **eye level**, with the lens level with your eyes. Do not shoot from above or below.
- [ ] Turn on Do Not Disturb and close other apps so no notifications appear.

## Camera framing

- [ ] **Clip 01 (medium close-up):** about 1 to 1.5 m (3 to 5 ft) from the phone. Head and shoulders in frame, eyes in the upper third.
- [ ] **Clip 02 (close-up):** about 0.6 to 0.9 m (2 to 3 ft) from the phone. Face fills about two-thirds of the frame height.
- [ ] Keep the framing the same for both clips.
- [ ] Leave a little headroom above your head. Do not crop the top of your head.

## Lighting

- [ ] Use a **soft key light** (a window, or a softbox or lamp with a diffuser) at about **45 degrees** to one side of your face.
- [ ] Add a **weaker fill light** (about half as bright) on the other side to lift shadows.
- [ ] Do not put a bright light or window behind you. It turns you into a dark silhouette.
- [ ] Check the face on the live preview. Eyes and cheeks should be clearly lit, with no harsh hot spots on the forehead.

## Background

- [ ] Use a **plain, dark wall** close to the colour `#0B1426` (Midnight Navy). This blends with the generated footage.
- [ ] Remove clutter, logos, screens, and bright objects behind you.
- [ ] Keep the background at least 1 m (3 ft) behind you so it stays soft and out of focus.

## Sound

- [ ] Record in a quiet room. Turn off fans, air conditioning, and music.
- [ ] Place a clip-on mic about 20 cm (8 in) from your mouth, or use a phone mic close to you.
- [ ] Do a 5-second test. Play it back and check that your voice is clear and not clipping.

## Delivery

- [ ] Read the script aloud once before recording. Mark the words you want to stress.
- [ ] Look at the lens, not the screen.
- [ ] Take a slow breath before you start. Smile slightly on "I'm" and "DevKit".
- [ ] Say each sentence at a steady pace. Pause at the commas and full stops marked in the script.
- [ ] Do a full take, then a second take if you want one. Pick the one that sounds most natural.

## Clean beginnings and endings

- [ ] Press record, wait **about 1 second in silence**, then start speaking.
- [ ] After your last word, stay still and silent for **about 1 second**, then stop recording.
- [ ] Do not move your head or change expression before the start or after the end. This makes the cut invisible.
- [ ] Record each clip as its own file. Do not record clip 01 and clip 02 in one take.
- [ ] Name the files clearly: `clip-01-intro.mov` and `clip-02-explain.mov`.

## Checks before you finish

- [ ] Clip 01 is about 12 s long, with speech of about 4.5 to 5.0 s.
- [ ] Clip 02 is about 10 s long, with speech of about 3.0 to 3.5 s.
- [ ] Your face is sharp, lit evenly, and in the same framing as the other clip.
- [ ] You can hear your voice clearly with no background noise.
- [ ] The start and end are silent for about 1 second each.
- [ ] Both files play back correctly before you move on to editing.
