# DevKit Founder Recording Script

Read this before you record. Your two clips sit inside the generated video. Record each one separately, then drop it into the timeline in [editing-timeline.md](./editing-timeline.md).

## Before you record

- **Replace `[your name]` with your name.**
- **Check the origin line.** The repository does not say why you built DevKit. The line "my AI agents kept forgetting my project" is a placeholder for your real reason. Change it to something that is true for you. Do not say anything you cannot back up.
- **Speak naturally.** Short sentences are easier to say on camera. Pause where a comma or full stop is marked.
- **Target pace:** about 2.5 words per second. Do not rush the last word.

## Clip 01: Founder introduction

- **Timestamp in final video:** 4.10 s to 10.38 s (slot length 6.28 s).
- **Words to speak:**

  > I'm [your name]. [pause] I built DevKit because my AI agents kept forgetting my project.

- **Approximate recording duration:** about 12 s total. Include about 1 s of silence at the start and about 1 s at the end, then the speech.
- **Camera framing:** medium close-up, head and shoulders, eyes in the upper third of the frame.
- **Facial expression and delivery:** warm and direct. Start with a slight smile. Say "I'm" and your name clearly, then a short pause. Slow down slightly on "my project". This is a person introducing their own work, not reading an ad.
- **What appears on screen while you speak:** the plate behind the clip is a plain Midnight Navy background with a small DevKit corner mark. Your clip covers the full frame, so the plate is hidden.
- **Face visible or product footage:** your face stays on screen for the full slot. At 10.38 s the video cuts to the product reveal (wordmark and tagline).

## Recording rules for both clips

- Look at the lens, not the screen.
- Start talking about one second after you press record. Stop about one second after you finish.
- Do not turn away between takes.
- Keep the same position, lighting, and framing for both clips so they match when cut together.
- Keep the first and last second silent so the cut is clean.
